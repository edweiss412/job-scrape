### 1. RESUME TAILORING

**ADD (Technical Proficiencies - Top of Section):**

*Why:* The ATS will auto-reject you without these acronyms appearing near the top. Even if your experience is "Event-based," you've used these to bridge remote presenters. You need to claim the territory.

**REWRITE (Professional Profile):**
**BEFORE:** "A1 audio engineer with 8+ years of experience spanning large-scale festival/concert production and high-end corporate AV..."

Expert in resolving complex signal flow issues in real-time for Fortune 500 leadership. Combines deep technical audio roots with a background in Accounting to align AV operations with corporate business objectives."

*Why:* Voya is a financial firm. Leading with "festivals" makes you look like a flight risk who just wants to go back on tour. Leading with "Executive Suites" and "Accounting" makes you a "culture fit."

**REWRITE (Experience - Freelance Title):**
**BEFORE:** "Freelance Audio Engineer"
**AFTER:** "AV Systems Engineer & Event Lead (Contract)"

*Why:* "Freelance" sounds hobbyist to a corporate HR screener. "Systems Engineer" matches the job title they are hiring for. 

**REWRITE (Experience - Bullet 1):**
**BEFORE:** "Deliver A1 mixing, RF coordination, and PA deployment across ~70 events per year—corporate general sessions (up to 5,000 attendees)..."
**AFTER:** "Provide Tier-4 technical support and real-time AV engineering for executive-level meetings and hybrid events. Specialized in the rapid resolution of hardware/software failures in high-pressure environments, ensuring 100% uptime for C-Suite presentations."

*Why:* They used the phrase "4.0 level" troubleshooting and "fast-track" resolution. This wording mirrors their internal language for high-level support.

**REWRITE (Experience - Bullet 3):**
**BEFORE:** "Served as RF engineer for the 2025 WNBA All-Star Game... requiring full arena RF coordination."
**AFTER:** "Managed complex RF coordination and wireless microphone deployments for national broadcast environments (WNBA All-Star 2025); expertise in Shure Axient Digital and Wireless Workbench ensures zero-interference communication in RF-congested urban environments like Manhattan."

*Why:* NYC is the hardest RF environment in the world. Highlighting that you can handle 128 channels in an arena proves you can handle their boardroom wireless systems without breaking a sweat.

**REWRITE (Glades Music Festival - Leadership):**
**BEFORE:** "Manage a crew of 5–6 audio technicians and stagehands per event..."
**AFTER:** "Supervise and mentor a technical team of 6; developed standardized SOPs, technical workflows, and training modules to elevate Team Level 1–2.5 support capabilities. Managed end-to-end decommissioning and rebuild of festival audio infrastructure."

*Why:* The job description specifically asks for someone who can "help train the AV-Desktop support team." This proves you have the "Manager/Mentor" DNA they want.

**REWRITE (Education):**
**BEFORE:** "Bachelor of Science in Accounting | West Virginia University"
**AFTER:** "Bachelor of Science in Accounting | West Virginia University (Strong Financial Services Business Acumen / Budget Management)"

*Why:* At Voya, everyone is a "financial person." Your degree is a massive "secret weapon" that proves you understand their business (compliance, ROI, budgets) better than a standard roadie.

2.  **Hybrid Latency:** "How do you manage echo cancellation and gain-before-feedback when a VIP is presenting via Teams in a room with a high-output PA?"
3.  **MTR Lifecycle:** "Talk us through a conference room 'de commissioned and rebuild.' How do you document the signal flow for the next guy?"
4.  **RF Complexity:** "Midtown Manhattan is an RF nightmare. How do you use Wireless Workbench to guarantee a clean 10-mic setup for a Board meeting?"
5.  **Dante/IT:** "How would you troubleshoot a clocking issue on a Dante network that is causing intermittent pops in the CEO's boardroom?"

**Behavioral Questions:**
1.  **The "Pivot":** "You've been a freelancer for 8 years. Why Voya, and why a 5-day-a-week office role now?" (*Answer Focus: Transitioning from 'executing someone else's vision' to 'owning the infrastructure' and building long-term institutional value.*)
2.  **The Crisis:** "Tell me about a time a high-profile meeting was failing and you had seconds to fix it." (*Use the 'UAD Luna on zero notice' story, but frame it as 'saving the client's broadcast'.*)
3.  **The Mentor:** "How do you explain a complex signal flow concept to a Desktop Support tech who only knows how to reboot a PC?"
