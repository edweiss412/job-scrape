*Your answer should include:* Site survey methodology, spectrum scan before frequency assignment, antenna distribution strategy (distro vs. direct), Axient Digital's ShowLink for real-time monitoring, Wireless Workbench workflow, infrastructure requirements (cable runs, antenna placement relative to reflective surfaces), and handoff documentation for the end client or integrator.
