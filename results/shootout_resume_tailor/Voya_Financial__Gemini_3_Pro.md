### 3. INTERVIEW PREP

**LIKELY TECHNICAL QUESTIONS**
1.  **"How do you handle audio looping/echo in a Teams Room with external mics?"**
    *   *Answer:* Discuss Mix-Minus and AEC (Acoustic Echo Cancellation). Explain that the far-end audio cannot be sent back to the far-end.
2.  **"A CEO is about to start a presentation and the projector is purple. What do you do?"**
    *   *Answer:* 1. Check connections (HDMI handshake). 2. Check color space settings (RGB vs YPbPr). 3. Swap the cable. *Key:* emphasized doing this calmly without disrupting the room.
3.  **"We use Cisco Room Kits. If a touch panel is unresponsive, how do you troubleshoot without entering the room?"**
    *   *Answer:* "I would access the device via the web interface (IP address) or Cisco Control Hub to check status, reboot the peripheral, or check for PoE faults." (Even if you haven't done this, memorize this answer. It's standard).
4.  **"Explain the difference between a Zoom Meeting and a Zoom Webinar relative to AV inputs."**
    *   *Answer:* Discussing how you manage panelists vs attendees and audio isolation.
5.  **"What is your process for documenting a room issue?"**
    *   *Answer:* Focus on the "Ticket" system. You identify the issue, implement a workaround for the meeting, tag the equipment as "Out of Service," and log the ticket for repair/replacement.

**BEHAVIORAL & SITUATIONAL QUESTIONS**
1.  **"Why do you want to leave the freelance world for a 9-5 corporate job?"**
    *   *The Trap:* They think you'll get bored.
    *   *The Pivot:* "I've achieved my goals in live production—I've done the arenas, I've done the festivals. Now, I want to apply my Accounting background and my technical skills to build something long-term. I want a team, a home base, and to solve complex integration problems rather than just load-in/load-out."
2.  **"Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to a high-status client."**
    *   *Story:* Use the RF coordination story. "We had a frequency conflict. I didn't just say 'it won't work.' I presented Option A/B, explained the risk, and let them decide."
3.  **"How do you handle a user who thinks they know more about AV than you?"**
    *   *Strategy:* "I validate their input, but I frame my solution as 'ensuring the best broadcast quality for the remote participants.' People care more about how they look/sound to others."
