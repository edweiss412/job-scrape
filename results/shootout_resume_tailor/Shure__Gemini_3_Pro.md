### 3. INTERVIEW PREP

**LIKELY TECHNICAL QUESTIONS (The "Stump the Chump" Phase)**
*   **"Explain these 130 channels."** They will drill down on the AbbVie gig. How did you handle filtering? What bands were you in (G57, J5, etc.)? Did you use Quadversity? *Prep a 30-second technical deep dive that sounds effortless.*
*   **"How do you explain Dante clocking to an IT Manager?"** This is a classic test. You need to explain PTP (Precision Time Protocol) and Leader Clock without sounding condescending.
*   **The "MXA Gap":** They will ask about your experience with Shure's ceiling arrays (MXA920/902). *Answer:* "While my daily drivers are Axient and PSM for live events, I understand the physics of steerable lobes and beamforming. I am Dante Level 2 certified, so the backend integration is identical to what I already do. I can master the Designer software in a weekend."
*   **"How do you troubleshoot a dropout?"** Walk them through the signal chain logically: Transmitter > RF Environment > Antenna placement > Coax/cable > Distro > Receiver > Network. *Show them you have a process, not just guesses.*

**BEHAVIORAL/SITUATIONAL QUESTIONS**
*   **"Tell me about a time you had to tell a client their idea wouldn't work."**
    *   *Story:* Use the WNBA or AbbVie example. "The client wanted mics in a specific zone, but the noise floor was too high. I presented data (SMAART/WWB scan), explained the risk, and proposed an alternative antenna placement that satisfied their aesthetic needs without compromising the signal."
*   **"How do you handle a tech-illiterate stakeholder?"**
    *   *Story:* Use the "Executive Suites" experience. "When mixing for a CEO or Governor, I don't talk frequencies; I talk about 'clarity' and 'coverage.' I translate the tech into their user experience."

**THE FREELANCE-TO-PERM QUESTION**
*   **They will ask:** "You've been your own boss for 8 years. Why stick yourself in a corporate office now?"
*   **The Answer:** "I love the adrenaline of live shows, but I find myself most engaged during the *design* and *planning* phase. I want to move away from the physical setup and focus purely on the architecture of complex audio solutions. Shure allows me to do that on a global scale that I can't achieve as a single freelancer."

**QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM**
*   "In this territory, what is the ratio of direct end-user engagement (corporate clients) versus supporting Integrators/Dealers? Should I be prepared to sell *to* the channel or *through* the channel?" (This shows you understand B2B sales dynamics).
*   "With the convergence of AV and IT, how is Shure positioning itself against software-based appliance competitors in the conference room space?" (Shows industry awareness).
*   "Does this role have input into the product feedback loop? As a power user, I have insights on Wireless Workbench that I’d love to share with engineering." (Shows you are a team player).
