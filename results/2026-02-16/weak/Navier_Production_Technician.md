### 2. MATCH SCORE
**🔴 WEAK MATCH**
The candidate is a highly skilled **Live Sound Engineer (A1)**. This job is for a **Manufacturing/Assembly Technician** building physical boats. While there is a microscopic overlap in "wiring," the core competencies are entirely different.

### 3. REQUIREMENTS ALREADY MET
- **Build and route electrical harnesses / Wiring:** Candidate has experience with "30–40 channels of Riedel comms" and "deploying 130-channel wireless," which involves complex signal flow and physical cable management.
- **Use hand tools and shop equipment:** Candidate "stages, preps, and maintains $3M+ in rental inventory" and "assists with array flying and rigging," implying familiarity with basic shop tools and safety.
- **Documentation:** Candidate possesses a BS in Accounting; the job requires "production tracking through documentation and basic MRP input."

### 4. REQUIREMENTS WITH EXPERIENCE BUT NOT HIGHLIGHTED
- **Soldering:** As an A1/Systems Tech at a shop like Technotrix, the candidate almost certainly knows how to solder XLR or repair cables. This could be highlighted as a transferrable "precision electronics" skill.
- **Technical Schematics:** The candidate reads signal flow diagrams and Dante network maps. This translates to reading electrical schematics for a boat.

### 5. TRUE GAPS
- **Mechanical Assembly/Marine Tech (Critical):** The candidate has zero experience with high-voltage EV battery systems, plumbing, propulsion, or foils.
- **Composite Fabrication (Critical):** This role heavily emphasizes lamination, gelcoat, fairing, and resin work. Stagehands/Audio Engineers do not do this.
- **Industry Knowledge (Major):** No experience with ABYC maritime standards.
- **Forklift/Crane (Nice-to-have):** Candidate "assists" with rigging but is not a certified operator.

### 7. VERDICT
1. **Should I apply?** **No.** This is a manufacturing job, not an AV/Events job. You are overqualified in leadership/intelligence but underqualified in the specific trade skills (composites/mechanics) required.
2. **Is it worth tailoring my resume?** **No.** Unless you are looking to exit the entertainment industry entirely to become a boat builder, this is a poor fit.
3. **What's the single most important thing to change or add if tailoring?** You would need to emphasize your Technotrix shop experience and your Accounting degree's attention to detail, framing yourself as a "Systems Assembly" expert rather than a "Creative/Mix" expert.
