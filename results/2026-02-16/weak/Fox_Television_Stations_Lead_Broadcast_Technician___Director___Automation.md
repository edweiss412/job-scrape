### 2. MATCH SCORE
**Rating: 🔴 WEAK MATCH**
*The candidate is a high-level Live Sound/RF Engineer. This role is for a Television Technical Director (TD). While both use the word "technical," the skill sets are fundamentally different. A TD operates a video switcher (like a Ross Carbonite or Sony XVS) and manages automation software (like Ross OverDrive or Grass Valley Ignite) to "cut" a news broadcast. The candidate’s resume is almost 100% focused on audio engineering and RF coordination.*

### 3. REQUIREMENTS ALREADY MET
*   **Good Interpersonal Skills:** Demonstrated by "Trusted repeat relationships with 7–8 production companies" and "Direct mixing credits for a former U.S. President."
*   **Communication with Talent:** Experience "managing artist wrangling" and working directly with Fortune 500 executive suites.
*   **Flexible Schedule:** The candidate is a freelancer "delivering ~70 events per year," implying comfort with the non-traditional hours mentioned in the posting.

### 4. REQUIREMENTS WITH EXPERIENCE BUT NOT HIGHLIGHTED
*   **Signal Flow Knowledge:** The candidate lists a "philosophy" of strong signal flow. In a broadcast environment, understanding how video and audio signals route through a facility is vital. This could be highlighted, but it doesn't bridge the gap to operating a video switcher.
*   **Basic Video Support:** The resume mentions "basic video support (projectors, screens, simple switchers)." If these were Ross or Blackmagic switchers, it's a start, but the posting implies high-level automated news production.

### 5. TRUE GAPS
*   **Production Switchers (Video):** This is the core of the job. The candidate is an expert on *audio* consoles (Yamaha, Allen & Heath) but has no listed experience on *video* production switchers (Ross, Grass Valley, Sony). **Critically high gap/Dealbreaker.**
*   **News Automation:** The title specifically mentions "Automation." This refers to coding/triggering newsroom automation (Ross OverDrive/Ignite). The candidate’s experience is in live "seat-of-the-pants" mixing, not programmed broadcast automation. **Critically high gap.**
*   **Broadcast Directing:** The role involves directing/assisting in directing newscasts. The candidate’s leadership is in stage management and audio crew leads, not the aesthetic and timing-driven world of broadcast television.

1.  **Should I apply?** **No.** This is a pivot into a different craft (Video/Automation) rather than a progression of your current craft (Audio/RF).
2.  **Is it worth tailoring my resume?** **No.** You would need to fabricate years of video switching experience to be competitive.
3.  **What's the single most important thing to change or add if tailoring?** If you were determined to pivot, you would need to highlight the "Simple Switchers" bullet point and specifically name the hardware, emphasizing any experience with "Technical Directing" or "Vision Mixing."
