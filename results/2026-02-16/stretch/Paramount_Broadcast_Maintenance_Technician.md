### 5. TRUE GAPS
*   **Broadcast-Specific Hardware:** You lack experience with Sony switchers, Grass Valley NLEs, Evertz routers, and Harmonic encoders. These are the "bones" of a TV station.
*   **Transmitters:** You have experience with PA "transmitters" (RF mics/IEMs), but this role requires high-power TV transmitters (Klystrons, IOTs, cooling systems). This is a heavy electrical/mechanical gap.
*   **FCC Regulations:** You know RF coordination, but not necessarily the legal/logging requirements of a broadcast license holder.
*   **Component-Level Repair:** Most live sound techs swap a cable or a box; maintenance techs open the box and replace the capacitor. If you don't enjoy a soldering iron, you will hate this job.
