### 10. INTERVIEW PREP

1.  **"How do you handle a scenario where a CEO is on a Zoom call in the Boardroom and the audio drops?"** (Answer: Check the local mute, check the DSP signal flow, check the network. Remain calm. Do not blame the gear.)
2.  **"What is your experience with Crestron or Control systems?"** (Answer: "I operate them daily. I am not a programmer, but I can troubleshoot the signal flow *to* the processor and work effectively with your integrator if code changes are needed.")
3.  **"We have a Town Hall next week. How would you plan it?"** (Answer: Walk me through the run of show. Who is the talent? Do we need a mix-minus for remote? Do we need RF coordination? I create a channel list and a backup plan.)
4.  **"Explain Dante to a non-technical Project Manager."** (Answer: "It's like Excel for audio cables. Instead of running physical heavy copper wires, we send digital channels over a network cable, allowing us to route audio anywhere in the building instantly.")
