## Company Assessment

Lehigh Valley Productions is a legitimate regional AV rental and production company serving the Allentown/Bethlehem/Easton corridor in Pennsylvania, with reach into the Poconos, Philadelphia suburbs, and New Jersey. They cover a solid range of event types — concerts, corporate, school/university events, weddings, and block parties — and operate their own gear inventory including EAW, RCF, DAS line arrays, QSC amps, and Allen & Heath mixers. The business appears to be owner-operated with a small crew and has been active for at least 10 years based on their own claims.

The core problem for Eric is geography. Chicago to the Lehigh Valley is not a day call — it requires a flight, which eats into the economics of a single-day gig unless the client is paying full travel costs. While Eric does have Pennsylvania roots (Glades Music Festival in Gibbon Glade, PA; Pittsburgh-area venues on his resume), those are western PA connections, not eastern PA. The Lehigh Valley market is entirely separate. Without a regional base or existing relationships there, this would be a cold outreach into a market where he has no foothold.

On the gear side, there's limited overlap with Eric's core competencies. His value proposition is built on L-Acoustics K2/KARA, large-scale RF coordination, and high-stakes corporate work. Lehigh Valley Productions is working with QSC, EAW, and DAS — solid mid-tier gear, but not the profile of a company that would specifically seek out an A1 with arena-scale RF chops and L-Acoustics deployment experience. The ceiling here is a 3,000-person event, which is within Eric's range but well below where his skills are most differentiated.

## Why They Would Want the Candidate
