## Company Assessment

AAR (aarental.com) is a small, legitimate AV rental and event services company based in Philadelphia serving the B2B market — primarily nonprofits, government agencies, and meeting planners. Their operation is centered on equipment delivery, setup, and on-site tech support for corporate meetings, town halls, graduations, and similar events. They emphasize reliability (pre-staging backup gear, sending a tech for the full event duration) and appear to run a lean crew model with in-house staff like "Oscar," "Jose," and "Darren" handling on-site work.

The core problem for Eric is scale and gear mismatch. Their inventory highlights are video walls, projectors, laptops, portable stages, truss systems, and tour guide systems — the audio component is mentioned generically as "Sound Systems for Events" with no brand specificity beyond a passing reference to RF and SSL. There is no indication they deploy L-Acoustics, d&b, Meyer, or any of the professional PA systems Eric specializes in, and no sign they hire dedicated A1 freelancers at his experience tier versus generalist AV techs who deliver and operate everything.

Geography compounds the issue. Philadelphia is a viable destination for a multi-day corporate gig with the right production company, but this company doesn't appear to be running the kind of high-stakes, high-budget events that would justify flying in a senior A1 from Chicago. They appear to service the lower-to-mid tier of the corporate event market.
