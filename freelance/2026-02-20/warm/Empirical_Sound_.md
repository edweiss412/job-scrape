## Company Assessment

They serve local musicians, DJs, churches, schools, and event clients across the Cleveland and Columbus area, offering PA rental, backline, stage, lighting, DJ production, and equipment repair. Their physical showroom and authorized service center credentials (EV, Peavey, Yamaha, JBL, Roland, and many others) speak to a legitimate, functioning business with deep local roots.

The business model appears to center on rentals, repair services, and local DJ/band gigs rather than the kind of A1 day-call freelance production work Eric does routinely.
