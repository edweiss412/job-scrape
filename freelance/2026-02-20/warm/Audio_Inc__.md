## Company Assessment

Audio Inc. is a 30-year-old full-service audio rental, production, and installation company based in Union, NJ — squarely in the NYC metro corridor. They serve festivals, theaters, corporations, museums, schools, and houses of worship, and they explicitly offer production staffing alongside their rental operation. Their gear inventory includes recognizable professional brands (Yamaha, Allen & Heath, Avid, Martin Audio, Shure, Sennheiser, Clear-Com) that align reasonably well with Eric's experience, even if they're not running L'Acoustics K2 rigs at arena scale. The company appears to operate at a mid-to-large regional level with a diverse event calendar.

The core problem here is geography. Union, NJ is approximately 800 miles from Chicago — a full domestic flight away. For a Chicago-based freelancer, this company offers zero day-call viability under normal circumstances. Multi-day gigs are theoretically possible, but only if the rate, scope, and logistics justify the travel overhead. Without an existing relationship or a compelling reason to be in the NYC area, this company sits in the "possible but impractical" category.

That said, the NYC metro is one of the highest-density live event markets in the country. If Eric is ever traveling to the region — or looking to expand his geographic footprint — Audio Inc. would be a reasonable company to have in his network. Their staffing model, 30-year track record, and diverse client base suggest they do hire freelancers and value experienced engineers. It's a warm lead worth a cold outreach email, not a priority target.
