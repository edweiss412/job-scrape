## Company Assessment

The Music Trust is a Chicago-based (and multi-metro) event production and entertainment company that positions itself as a full-service shop covering music events, corporate production, and live events ranging from 75 to 75,000 attendees. Their website lists real venue names (Roof on theWit, Moxy Chicago, Lizzies Starlight Room in San Francisco) and covers the types of events — corporate holiday parties, speaking engagements, product launches, training sessions — that would naturally require a skilled A1. Their Chicago presence is a genuine geographic plus for Eric.

That said, the company reads more like a boutique creative/entertainment agency than a true production company. The inclusion of DJs, talent booking agents, wedding production, and milestone birthday events alongside corporate AV suggests a lighter technical operation that likely leans on rental houses and subcontractors rather than deploying its own pro-audio infrastructure. The sole gear mention of SSL — absent any context around L-Acoustics, DiGiCo, Shure Axient, or even Yamaha CL-series consoles — is a thin signal and doesn't clearly align with Eric's resume profile. The scale claims (up to 75,000 people) are not substantiated by visible evidence on the site.

The 20+ years of corporate experience claim and multi-city footprint give some credibility, but without verifiable large-scale credits, named clients, or staff engineers visible, it's hard to assess how much A1-level freelance work they actually generate versus how much they hand off to DJs or lighter operators.
