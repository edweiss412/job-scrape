Their core business is AV system design, installation, and integration across verticals including mission-critical control rooms, courtroom AV, house of worship, corporate conferencing, and sports/broadcast — with event production listed as one service line among many. The Peoria/Morton IL operation is actually somewhat geographically proximate to Chicago, but it's install-focused, not event-focused.
