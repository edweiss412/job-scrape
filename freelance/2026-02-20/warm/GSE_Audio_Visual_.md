## Company Assessment

GSE Audio Visual is a multi-location AV rental and production company with offices in Las Vegas, a corporate HQ in Suwanee, GA, and apparent roots in the trade show and convention space. They hold EAC credentials with the Las Vegas Convention Center, which is a real and meaningful industry certification, and their client testimonials reference legitimate trade shows (CES, PGA Merchandise Show, NPE, NASP). They are a functioning business — not a directory listing — and appear to serve the exhibitor-appointed contractor niche at major convention venues.

That said, the company's core value proposition is trade show booth AV support — primarily screens, LED walls, and computer rentals for exhibitors — rather than large-scale live audio production. The website references generic "Audio Rentals" as a service category but provides no specifics on consoles, PA systems, or RF infrastructure. Nothing in their profile suggests they deploy L-Acoustics hangs, run complex RF coordination, or mix large corporate general sessions at the scale Eric handles. Their sweet spot appears to be the exhibitor services market, which is a legitimate corner of the AV industry but not where an experienced A1 typically finds day-call work.

The geographic reality is also a significant drag. Eric is Chicago-based and would need to fly to Las Vegas for any engagement with this company. For multi-day trade show builds that could work — trade shows often span several days — but GSE's apparent audio depth doesn't suggest they'd be pulling in an A1 at Eric's level. The testimonials skew toward project management and logistics praise, not technical audio excellence, which signals their clients aren't demanding high-end mixing talent.
