However, a company of this size and longevity almost certainly has a tier of corporate, entertainment, and large-scale events that would suit Eric's skillset.

Worth a cold outreach given the geographic proximity and clear event volume. The goal would be to qualify what their audio production tier actually looks like — specifically whether they're doing corporate general sessions, concerts, or large-scale corporate galas that warrant an A1 of Eric's caliber versus smaller social events handled by a single operator running a QSC-based rig.
