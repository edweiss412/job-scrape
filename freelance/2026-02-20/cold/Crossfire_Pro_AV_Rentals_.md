## Company Assessment

Crossfire Pro AV Rentals is a legitimate full-service event production and AV rental company based in Brooklyn, NY, serving the Northeast US corridor (NY, NJ, CT, PA). They operate a mobile stage fleet and rent everything from lighting and backline to PA systems and staging. Their website is real, functional, and transactional — customers can browse and quote-request directly online, suggesting an active operation with steady regional event volume.

The core problem for Eric is the gear tier. Their featured audio inventory consists of Fender Passport systems (150W–500W), LD Systems Maui 28, and basic powered speaker packages priced at $125–$275. There is no mention of professional consoles (Yamaha CL, Allen & Heath dLive, DiGiCo), no line array systems, no RF coordination infrastructure, and no Dante networking — essentially none of the gear ecosystem where Eric operates. This is a company serving small-to-mid-size events where a skilled A1 is likely overkill, and where a competent A2 or even a tech-savvy generalist could handle the audio needs.

The geographic gap compounds the issue. Brooklyn is not an impractical destination — Chicago to NYC flights exist — but there is no existing relationship, no compelling reason Eric would be preferred over a qualified local New York freelancer, and the gear level doesn't justify the travel expense. Even if Crossfire occasionally handles a larger corporate show requiring real audio chops, their catalog suggests that's not their lane.
