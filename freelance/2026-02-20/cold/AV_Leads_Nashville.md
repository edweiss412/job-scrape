## Company Assessment

AV Leads (avleads.com) is an AV labor and crewing placement service that operates across dozens of US cities, with Nashville listed as one of its primary markets. The company positions itself as a connector between AV technicians and production companies, rental houses, venues, and meeting planners—essentially functioning as a labor broker or staffing intermediary rather than a direct production company that owns gear, books shows, and hires A1s directly. The website has a functional, professional structure, but is very light on specific clients, events, gear, or operational depth, which makes it difficult to assess true scale or quality of placements.

From Eric's perspective, Nashville is roughly 5 hours by car or a short flight—workable for multi-day corporate events or festivals, but not a market he can easily tap for day calls without travel overhead eating into margin. AV Leads' multi-city footprint (notably including Chicago, Indianapolis, and other Midwest cities) is more interesting than the Nashville listing alone, and it's worth noting that a relationship with a national labor broker like this could theoretically generate calls across multiple markets. However, staffing intermediaries typically compress rates and add a layer between Eric and the actual production company relationship he needs to build.

The event types listed—corporate conferences, breakout rooms, expo halls, trade shows, festivals—are a strong content match for Eric's background, including his RF coordination and corporate A1 work. But without any named clients, confirmed gear stack, or evidence of high-end production scale, this feels like a broad-net labor platform rather than a targeted, high-value relationship.
