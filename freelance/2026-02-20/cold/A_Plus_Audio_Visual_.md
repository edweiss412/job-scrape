Their offerings span speaker rentals, projector/screen packages, LED video walls, staging, lighting, and DJ equipment — primarily targeting schools, houses of worship, weddings, corporate meetings, and small concerts. They appear to be a solid regional operator at the smaller end of the production spectrum.

There's no indication of national reach, touring work, or any reason they'd pull a freelancer from the Midwest. Even if they did reach out, the travel overhead would price the day rate out of range for the kinds of events they service.
