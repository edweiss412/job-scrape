## Company Assessment

Stage Lights and Sound is a full-service equipment rental and production company based in Richmond, CA, serving the greater San Francisco Bay Area. Their inventory spans portable staging, event lighting (Martin Mac 700, Mac 2000 Profile fixtures), pipe and drape, LED video walls, tent and power solutions, and basic audio gear (speakers, subwoofers, stage monitors, DJ equipment). They position themselves as a one-stop shop for corporate AV, festivals, and community events across Northern California.

The fundamental problem for Eric is geographic: at 2,100+ miles from Chicago, this company cannot support day calls in any practical sense. The audio rental inventory reads more like a regional general-event rental house than a production company deploying arena-class systems.

There's nothing wrong with this company as a business — they appear legitimate and active — but they simply don't operate in Eric's market, at Eric's scale, or with Eric's gear ecosystem. No outreach is recommended.
