## Company Assessment
Audio Video West is a real, established AV rental and production company based in West Los Angeles (11723 Mississippi Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90025), operating since 1990. They serve a broad range of event types including corporate meetings, pharma events, concerts, conventions, and private parties — which on paper overlaps with Eric's work portfolio. They offer AV operator and technician services in addition to equipment rental, so there is a theoretical path to crewing up freelancers.

However, the company operates almost entirely within the greater Los Angeles metro area, making it a non-starter for a Chicago-based freelancer seeking day calls. The gear footprint is decidedly prosumer — Shure ULXD wireless (not Axient Digital), generic PA rentals, projectors, and flat screens — with no mention of L-Acoustics, professional digital consoles, or any of the large-format infrastructure that defines Eric's work. The scale of events they service appears to be small-to-mid corporate and social, not the 5,000-attendee general sessions or arena RF deployments in Eric's portfolio.

Even if the distance weren't a factor, this company doesn't appear to regularly work at a scale or technical level that would make good use of Eric's A1 credentials, RF coordination expertise, or system optimization skills. It's a legitimate small business, but essentially a mismatch on every practical dimension.
