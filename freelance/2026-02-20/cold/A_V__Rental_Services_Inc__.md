## Company Assessment

A.V. Rental Services Inc. is a Philadelphia-based AV rental and production company founded in 2005 as a sole proprietorship, now serving corporate meetings, hotel events, pharma speaker trainings, houses of worship, and small-to-mid concerts. They are a legitimate, long-operating business with association memberships (MPI, PA Convention & Visitors Bureau) and video testimonials that speak to a real client base. That said, their positioning — with a pharma "headache-free rental package" starting at $200 including a projector, screen, and small sound system — signals a company operating firmly in the small-to-mid market, not the large-scale production space Eric occupies.

The gear language on their site is vague and never rises to the level of L-Acoustics, DiGiCo, Shure Axient Digital, Dante, or any system that would require an experienced A1 of Eric's caliber. Their concert sound offering mentions backline and staging but provides no specifics that suggest K2 hangs, large-format consoles, or RF coordination at scale. The work types overlap in name only — pharma AV, hotel meetings, and concerts are all on Eric's resume, but the execution tier here is several levels below his typical engagement.

Geography is a significant barrier. Philadelphia is a 2-hour flight from Chicago, making day calls economically unviable and multi-day gigs dependent on travel buy-in from both sides. Eric already has deep pharma and corporate AV relationships in Chicago and the Midwest — there is no compelling reason to pursue a small Philadelphia operator when the geographic and scale mismatch is this significant.
