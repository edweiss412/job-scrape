## Company Assessment

Maine Sound and Lighting Rentals operates under the Northeast Event Design umbrella out of Lisbon, Maine — a centralized but decidedly rural location serving Portland, Lewiston, Auburn, and Augusta. The company offers a full suite of rental services including sound, lighting, video, staging, and furniture, with both full-service delivery/setup and DIY weekend pickup options. Their business model is clearly oriented toward community events, weddings, school functions, and modest corporate work — not the large-scale production environment Eric thrives in.

The gear inventory tells the whole story: RCF HDL/NXL/Evox line arrays, Midas M32/M32R, QSC TouchMix, Sennheiser G3, and Shure ULXD are solidly prosumer-to-mid-tier tools. Eric knows these platforms (the M32 and QLXD appear on his resume as "proficient" secondary tools), but this is nowhere near the L-Acoustics K2, Shure Axient Digital, Yamaha CL/dLive ecosystem where he operates at his best. The scale ceiling here is a local festival or mid-sized wedding ballroom — not 5,000-person general sessions or arena RF deployments.

Even setting aside gear and scale, the geography is a dealbreaker. Maine has no major metro hub, and traveling from Chicago for a day call at a rental house of this size would be economically absurd. There is no realistic path to a recurring freelance relationship here.
