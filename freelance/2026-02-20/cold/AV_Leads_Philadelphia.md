## Company Assessment

AV Leads (avleads.com) is a national audio visual labor and crewing platform that connects AV technicians with production companies, rental houses, and event venues across dozens of US cities. Rather than being a traditional production company or AV rental firm with its own gear inventory and project pipeline, AV Leads functions as a staffing intermediary — essentially a marketplace or placement agency that fills technician roles for third-party clients. Their Philadelphia page is one of dozens of nearly identical city-specific landing pages, which is a red flag for depth of local operations.

From a geographic standpoint, Philadelphia is not a realistic day-call market for an engineer based in Chicago. Eric could theoretically register with AV Leads' travel team program, but staffing agencies of this type typically prioritize local technicians first. There is no gear information on the site whatsoever — no mention of L-Acoustics, DiGiCo, Shure Axient, or any other brand that would suggest work at Eric's level. The event types listed (corporate events, trade shows, breakout rooms, festivals) are conceptually aligned, but without gear or scale signals, it's impossible to know if the work is A1-level mixing or basic AV tech/breakout-room operator roles.

The credibility concern here is meaningful. AV Leads sits somewhere between a real staffing company and an aggregator page — it does appear to be an actual operating business with a national reach, but its model is fundamentally a labor pool, not a production partnership. For an engineer of Eric's caliber with 8+ years of experience, established production company relationships across 20+ vendors, and credits including Obama and the WNBA All-Star Game, registering with a staffing aggregator in a non-local city offers minimal strategic value.
