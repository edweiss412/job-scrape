2. MATCH SCORE

🔴 WEAK MATCH While you have elite live audio skills, this is an IT Infrastructure/Systems Administration role. The job is 10% "mixing/events" and 90% patching servers, managing VTC bridges (Cisco/Poly), and troubleshooting desktop VoIP software. You are an artist/engineer; they are looking for a SysAdmin with a CCNA.

3. REQUIREMENTS ALREADY MET

  • Bachelor’s Degree: You have a BS from WVU (Accounting), which satisfies the degree requirement.
  • LiveStream Events: You have extensive experience mixing and managing live signals for high-stakes clients (Obama, Fortune 500), supporting the "LiveStream events" bullet.
  • Audio Systems: "Display and projection systems... audio systems." Your 8+ years as an A1 and experience with basic video/projection meets the hardware side of this.
  • Communication: Your experience managing crews of 5-6 and working with high-level executives (CEOs, Governors) proves "excellent written and verbal communication."

4. REQUIREMENTS WITH EXPERIENCE BUT NOT HIGHLIGHTED

  • Digital Signage: You’ve worked in hotel environments (AbbVie) where digital signage is standard. You likely know how to troubleshoot a media player/display, but it’s a minor part of your resume.
  • Agile/DevOps: You haven't worked in a software dev environment, but "managing a crew of 5-6" and "building operations from the ground up" at the Glades Festival demonstrates the mentality of process improvement.

5. TRUE GAPS

  • IT Infrastructure Certification (The Dealbreaker): They want a CCNA Collaboration. You have Dante and SMAART certifications, which are high-end audio, but not Cisco-specific Networking certifications.
  • UC Software Stack: The job requires deep admin experience in Cisco Call Manager (CUCM), Expressways, Polycom DMA, and vBrick. These are backend server tools, not live consoles.
  • Federal Clearance: You do not currently hold an active DHS Public Trust/EOD. While "able to obtain" is listed, many contractors prefer those who already have it.
  • Network Fax/VoIP: This is "telephony" work (Skype for Business, Avaya, eFax). Most live A1s find this work incredibly tedious.
